Contact

Contact Loona Automation

Contact Loona Automation: email loonaautomation@gmail.com, hire the studio on Fiverr, or message @loonaautomation — every enquiry is answered personally by the founder.

Every enquiry to Loona Automation is read and answered personally by the founder, Aurang Zeb Loona. There is no sales team and no ticket queue — describe the work you repeat most, and you will get a reply with first thoughts, a discovery step, and then a clear plan with a fixed quote before anything is built or charged.

Email — best for project briefs

Write to loonaautomation@gmail.com. Email suits detailed briefs best: tell us what your business or school does, which task eats your time every week, which apps you already use — for example WhatsApp, Google Sheets, a form or a CRM — and what a good result would look like. The more concrete the example, the more useful the first reply will be. Most enquiries receive a personal reply within one to two working days.

Hire the studio on Fiverr

If you prefer a packaged service with set deliverables, timelines and buyer protection, you can order through Fiverr, where the studio trades as @loonaautomation: fiverr.com/loonaautomation. LinkedIn and social platforms

You can also message the Loona Automation company page on LinkedIn, or reach the studio on any of its public platforms — the handle is @loonaautomation on Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, TikTok and X, and the studio's public code and product catalogue live on GitHub. Social messages are welcome for questions, feedback about the free tools, and suggestions for new products.

What happens after you write

First, we look together at your current workflow and find the repetitive steps costing you the most time — this discovery step is free. Second, you receive a plain-English plan: what will be automated, which tools it will use, how long it will take and exactly what it will cost. Third, if you approve the plan, the automation is built and tested with real scenarios before it ever touches a real customer. Finally, you receive the finished system with a simple walkthrough, plus support to adjust it as your work grows.

Teachers and education enquiries

Teachers are especially welcome. Loona Automation's education products — Loona AI Notes, Loona Bolti Kitab and Loona Education — grew out of the founder's own classroom, and feedback from teachers directly shapes what is built next. If a free tool helped your class, or if your school needs lesson-plan, question-paper or spoken-textbook support, please write and say so.
